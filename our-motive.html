How To Use Crystal

  1. Add a habit or task in the dashboard input box.
  2. Choose Daily for repeated habits or Deadline for time-bound tasks.
  3. Mark tasks complete to maintain momentum and track consistency.
  4. Review your list every day and remove tasks that are no longer relevant.

Why This Is Necessary

Most goals fail because they stay too big and too vague. Habit tracking breaks large goals into small, repeatable actions you can actually complete. It also makes your progress visible, which strengthens discipline and keeps you accountable over time.

Crystal helps you focus on process over pressure. When you show up regularly, improvement becomes natural and sustainable.
